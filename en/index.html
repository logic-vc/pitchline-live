Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Pitch Line Live analyzes your voice only on your device. Your voice and recordings are never sent to a server and are not used for advertising. There is no sign-up, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

1. Information processed only on your device

  • Live view and practice — Sound from the microphone is analyzed on your device into pitch and loudness and drawn on the screen. The results stay in memory only briefly and are not saved.
  • Recording — The app records only when you press the record button (up to 40 seconds, or up to 5 minutes when Pro is turned on with a code). Only the latest recording is kept in the device's temporary folder, and it is deleted when you go back to the live view or restart the app.
  • Library — Only recordings you choose to keep, and their names, are stored in the app's storage on this device. They are excluded from automatic device (cloud) backup.
  • Codes — For a code you enter, only a hash (not the code itself) is stored on this device.
  • Settings — App settings such as microphone sensitivity and the live view's target note are stored only on this device.

This information is not sent to any server, cannot be viewed by us, and is not shared with third parties.

2. Export

Only when you tap "Export" on a result screen and choose an app yourself do we pass a pitch line image or a recording (WAV) to that app. The app never sends them over the internet on its own. After sharing, the policy of the app you chose applies.

3. Advertising (free use)

While you use the app for free, a Google AdMob banner ad is shown at the bottom of the screen. For this, the advertising SDK collects the information below.

Ads are requested muted. Your voice, recordings and analysis results are never passed to the advertising SDK.

4. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before it loads any ads. You can accept, decline, or manage your options. The app does not load ads until this choice has been made.

  • Legal basis — Your consent for storing or accessing information on your device and for personalised advertising. Where Google or its partners rely on legitimate interest for certain purposes, you can object to it in the consent message.
  • Change or withdraw consent — Tap the ⓘ (information) button in the app, then Ad privacy choices. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing before the withdrawal.
  • International transfers — Information collected by the advertising SDK may be processed by Google outside your country, under Google's safeguards described in its privacy policy.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone — Used for pitch analysis and recording. Without it, the main features of the app cannot be used.
  • Internet · Advertising ID — Used to load ads.

6. Retention and deletion

Recordings in your library stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app, and are removed immediately when deleted. Temporary recordings are deleted automatically as described in section 1. Information collected by the advertising SDK is retained under Google's policies.

7. Your rights

You can rename or delete recordings in the library. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names may differ by device). Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because the data we handle stays on your device, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app or in your device settings. For data collected by Google's advertising SDK, see the Google Privacy Policy above. For anything else, contact us at the email below.

8. Children

This app is not directed at children. If you are under the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country (for example, 13 in the United States or up to 16 in some EU countries), please use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and their effective date on this page first. Changes that add to the information we process will also be announced in the app.
